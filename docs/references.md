# Methods and comparison papers

These references identify algorithms and scientific comparisons. **No third-party author program is redistributed, reconstructed from an archive, or automatically downloaded here.** Our independently implemented DRAM baseline and author-program result records are labeled separately.

- Yang et al. (2026), sedimentary structure and short-period RFs, JGR: Solid Earth. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JB032180). This is the original Bayesian comparison discussed in the manuscript; obtain any author software separately from its authors under their terms.
- Yao et al. (2022), joint P-polarization / RF inversion, SRL. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1785/0220210292).
- Haario et al. (2006), delayed-rejection adaptive Metropolis. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11222-006-9438-0). Algorithm reference for our DRAM implementation.
- Duane et al. (1987), Hybrid Monte Carlo. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(87)91197-X).
- Hoffman & Gelman (2014), NUTS and adaptive HMC. [Article](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v15/hoffman14a.html). FlowRF uses fixed/jittered trajectory HMC, not a NUTS implementation.
- Vehtari et al. (2021), rank-normalized/folded R-hat and ESS. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1214/20-BA1221).
- Papamakarios et al. (2021), normalizing flows. [Article](https://jmlr.org/papers/v22/19-1028.html).
- Dinh et al. (2017), Real NVP affine couplings. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.08803).
- Vaswani et al. (2017), Transformer. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).
- Brocher (2005), elastic wavespeed/density relations used by the archived shallow-control comparison. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120050077).

The complete manuscript bibliography is in `reproducibility/references.bib`; it contains bibliographic records, not article PDFs or source programs.
